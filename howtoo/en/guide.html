Guide

Five minutes to settle in

From the first launch to setting up your card payment date, in order.

Getting started

No sign-in required. Install it and start.

  1. Open the app and tap “Start with these”

    Every field is pre-filled with a reasonable value. Changing your take-home salary and monthly fixed costs is enough to begin. Everything else can be adjusted later in Settings.

  2. Check the accounts that were created

    The Assets tab already contains cash, a main bank account, a debit card and a credit card. Rename them and set your balances. Long-press to delete any you do not need.

  3. Enter your first transaction

    Tap the “+” button at the bottom right of the Ledger tab. Amount, category and account are all you need. Star an entry you use often and it becomes a one-tap entry next time.

  4. Paste a card approval SMS

    Paste the message from your card issuer into the paste field on the entry screen and the amount, merchant and date are filled in for you. The app never asks for permission to read your messages — you copy and paste it yourself.

Setting up a credit card payment date

This is the heart of Howtoo. Set it once and the payment date and amount are calculated for you from then on.

  1. Tap the card in the Assets tab

    When the account group is “Card”, fields for the payment date and billing period appear.

  2. Enter the payment date

    Use the date printed on your statement. For the 14th of each month, enter 14.

  3. Enter the day the billing period starts

    Read the billing period from your statement and enter the day it starts.

    Billing period on the statementValue to enter
    1st to the last day of the previous month1
    13th of last month to the 12th of this month13
    16th of last month to the 15th of this month16

    As you type, the app shows “this month’s billing period ○○ – ○○ · debited ○○” right below. Check it against your statement.

  4. Choose the payment account

    Pick the account the card bill is debited from. Setting this is what lets the app warn you when that account will be short on the payment date.

If the payment date is a weekend or holiday It is calculated as the next business day. That is the default for card payments, and you can change it per card.

Fixed costs and holiday rules

Money leaves in different ways depending on the item. You can express that difference directly.

SettingWhen to choose it
Same dayDirect debits such as insurance premiums and phone bills that leave the account on the day, weekend or not. (default)
Next business dayCard bills and some bank transfers. On a holiday they slide forward.
Previous business dayItems such as salary that arrive early when the date is a holiday.

Register these under Ledger > Fixed costs. As you enter one, “this month’s debit date ○○” appears immediately, and if a holiday pushes it into the following month, the app says “moves to next month”.

Turning on alerts

  1. Settings > Low balance alert for payment dates

    Turning the switch on asks for notification permission. Allow it and you are notified at 9 a.m. the day before the day you would run short.

  2. What you receive

    “On 26 October, 600,000 won leaves your main account. You are 100,000 won short.” Billing-period cutoffs are included too.

Alerts are built on the device too No amounts are sent to a server. The calculation and the scheduling both happen on your phone.

Using a spreadsheet on a PC (optional)

Keep a spreadsheet in Google Drive and sync it with the app in both directions.

  1. Settings > Spreadsheet ledger

    Connect a Google account and create or pick a file. The app can only reach the file it created — it does not see the rest of your Drive.

  2. Install Google Drive for desktop on your PC

    Open the same file in Excel, edit it and save. Wait for the Drive upload to finish, then sync in the app.

  3. Formulas work

    SUM, SUMIFS, IF and similar formulas in the amount column or a summary sheet are evaluated by the app. Formatting and charts are preserved.

Do not edit on the PC and in the app at the same time Saving an Excel file that has been open for a long time can make the Drive client overwrite a newer version. Open the current file, edit, save and close. If something goes wrong, Google Drive’s version history can restore it.

Backup and moving to a new device

  1. Settings > Export JSON

    Transactions, accounts, investments, journal entries and photos are saved into one file. Always export before changing devices.

  2. Import on the new device

    Settings > Import JSON restores everything. Spreadsheet connection details are deliberately excluded from the backup for security, so reconnect on the new device.

Uninstalling the app deletes your data Everything is stored on the device, so back up before you uninstall. There is no copy on our server.

If you get stuck

Lunar New Year 2027 isn’t marked as a holiday

Dates that move each year, such as Lunar New Year and Chuseok, need to be fetched. Use Settings > Holidays > Update public holidays, or add the date yourself on the same screen.

The projected payment doesn’t match my statement

The app only adds up the transactions you entered — it does not pull them from your card issuer. Check for missing entries first, then check that the billing period start day matches your statement.

I’m not getting alerts

Check that the switch is on in Settings and that notifications are allowed for Howtoo in your device settings. No alert is sent if the shortfall date has already passed or the balance is sufficient.

I want to delete my account

See Account and data deletion. If you only use the app, there is no account to delete — uninstalling removes your data with it.
