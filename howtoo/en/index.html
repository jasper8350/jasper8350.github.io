What other budget apps leave to you

When money actually moves depends on the calendar. That is where Howtoo starts.

Holidays

When the payment date is a holiday

A credit card bill slides to the next business day; an insurance direct debit goes out that same day regardless. You set the behaviour per item.

Month rollover

Next month’s expense, not this month’s

An end-of-month payment pushed to the 1st lands outside that month’s budget. Howtoo flags it when it happens.

Common questions

Does it import transactions from my bank?

No. Howtoo does not connect to bank or card-issuer accounts. There is nowhere in the app to store an account number, a card number, or brokerage credentials. It can, however, read a card approval SMS you paste in and fill the amount and merchant for you.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Export a JSON file from Settings and import it on the new device. If you turn on Google Drive spreadsheet sync, transactions and account names are matched between devices automatically.

Can I use it on a PC?

Turn on Drive sync and a spreadsheet appears in your own Google Drive. Open it in Excel, edit and save, and the app picks up the changes. Formulas in the amount column are evaluated by the app too.

Does it recommend what to invest in?

No. Howtoo is not an investment advisory service and never tells you to buy or sell anything. It calculates allocations and limits from figures you enter, as a reference for your own decisions.

How does it know the public holidays?

Weekends, fixed-date holidays and substitute holidays are computed by rule. Dates that move each year, such as Lunar New Year and Chuseok, are fetched from the Korean public data service or entered by you in Settings.
