Common questions

Does it import transactions from my bank?

No. Howtoo does not connect to bank or card-issuer accounts, and there is nowhere in the app to store an account number, a card number or brokerage credentials. It can read a card approval SMS you paste in and fill the amount and merchant for you.

Is the period tracking accurate?

It is an estimate based on the average of your past records. Cycles vary between people and between months, so it must not be relied upon for contraception or pregnancy planning. These records are stored on your device only and are never sent to our server or to Google Drive.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Export a JSON file from Settings and import it on the new device. With Google Drive sync on, transactions and account names are matched between devices automatically.

Can I use it on a PC?

Turn on Drive sync and a spreadsheet appears in your own Google Drive. Open it in Excel, edit, save, and the app picks up the changes — formulas included.

Does it recommend what to invest in?

No. Howtoo is not an investment advisory service and never tells you to buy or sell. It calculates allocations and limits from figures you enter, as a reference for your own decisions.

How does it know the public holidays?

Weekends, fixed-date holidays and substitute holidays are computed by rule. Dates that move each year, such as Lunar New Year and Chuseok, are fetched from the Korean public data service or entered by you in Settings.
